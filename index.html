🛂 Ooty E-Pass — Mandatory, Free

Apply Before You Enter Bandipur

As of May 2026, the e-Pass is mandatory year-round for all non-TN-43 vehicles entering Nilgiris district. It's free, takes 5 minutes, and the QR code is shown at the Masinagudi checkpost. Mobile signal is nearly zero inside Bandipur — apply at home or before Gundlupet. One e-Pass per vehicle. Download the QR as PDF or screenshot.

→ epass.tnega.org
  • 1. Visit epass.tnega.org
  • 2. Select "Nilgiris District"
  • 3. Enter vehicle number, travel date, passenger count
  • 4. Upload RC book image
  • 5. Submit → get QR code instantly
  • 6. Screenshot/PDF the QR — no internet needed at checkpost
  • 7. Pay Green Tax in cash at checkpost (~₹25–50/bike)

⚠️ Risk Assessment

☔ June Monsoon Risks

  • Ooty in June is wet — always. Rain gear is non-negotiable.
  • Sigur Ghat roads can be slippery — moss on tarmac.
  • Reduced visibility in Nilgiri fog. Use low beam, ride slow.
  • Pykara Falls may be swollen — don't enter the water.
  • Check road conditions at: https://tnroads.gov.in before Day 1.

🐘 Bandipur Wildlife Risks

  • Elephant herds cross NH 766 unpredictably — especially mornings.
  • If an elephant is on the road: stop engine, keep distance, wait.
  • Forest officials may hold you if herd is spotted — factor 30-min buffer.
  • Do not overtake or honk inside the forest stretch.

🏍️ Rider Safety Tips

  • Break every 90 minutes. Bandipur + ghat stretch = fatigue zone.
  • Full-face helmets mandatory — rain + bugs on ghats.
  • Hunter 350 handles ghats comfortably. Scram 450 — watch tyre pressure on wet roads.
  • Avoid overtaking on any ghat hairpin bend.
  • Hunter: keep to ~120 km/h max on highway. Scram: comfortable up to 130.

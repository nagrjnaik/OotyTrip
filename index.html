⚠️ Risk & Prep

☔ Monsoon Riding Rules

  • Wet tarmac = 50% more braking distance.
  • Low beam only in fog. High beams blind you.
  • Drop tyre pressure by 2-3 PSI for wet ghat grip.
  • Engine braking is mandatory downhill.

🎒 Pillion Mechanics

  • Lean WITH the rider. Don't fight physics.
  • Hold the grab rail, not the rider's shoulders.
  • Feet strictly on pegs. Never dangle feet.
  • Don't look down valley drops to avoid panic.

Documents & Digital

  • Driving Licence (Original)
  • RC Book (Original)
  • Valid Insurance & PUC
  • Ooty e-Pass QR (Offline)
  • Offline Google Maps (Mysore + Ooty)

Bike & Gear

  • Full-Face Helmets (Both)
  • Rain Gear / Waterproof Jackets
  • Tyre Inflator & Toolkit
  • Garbage bags (waterproofing luggage)
  • Power bank 10,000 mAh+
